FAQ

Common questions from buyers.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Standard catalogue items start from as few as 50 pieces per instrument type. OEM and custom manufacturing typically runs 200–500 pieces depending on complexity.

Do you manufacture under our own brand?

Yes — OEM and private-label manufacturing is a core service, including custom engraving, packaging and full regulatory documentation for your target market.

What certifications do your instruments carry?

ISO 9001, CE Mark and FDA registration, with compliance to EN ISO, ASTM and DIN standards. Full documentation is provided on request.

How long does shipping take?

2–4 weeks for catalogue items, 4–8 weeks for custom OEM orders. Express air freight, standard air freight and sea freight options are all available.

Can I become an authorised distributor?

Yes — exclusive territory arrangements, distributor pricing and marketing support are available. Reach out with your company details and target market.

What finishes are available?

Standard mirror/satin finish, gold-plated finish, and Tungsten Carbide (TC) tipped instruments for extended edge retention.

Do you provide product samples?

Yes, samples can be requested through the contact form or directly via WhatsApp before placing a bulk order.
