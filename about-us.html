OUR STORY

Fifty years on the factory floor.

Suddle Group started in Sialkot in 1976 and has grown into one of Pakistan's leading manufacturers of surgical and dental instruments, shipping to more than 30 countries.

TIMELINE

Five decades of manufacturing.

1976

Founded in Sialkot

Started as a small surgical instrument workshop in Pakistan's historic instrument-manufacturing district.

1990s

Export Expansion

Began shipping catalogue instruments to distributors across Europe and the Middle East.

2000s

Certification Milestones

Achieved ISO 9001 and CE certification, opening doors to hospital and clinic procurement worldwide.

2010s

Dental Range Launch

Expanded the catalogue to a full dental instrument range spanning nine specialties.

Today

900+ Combined Variants

Serving hospitals, clinics, distributors and OEM partners across 30+ countries.
